# Design System

## Typography

- **Display & Headlines:** Plus Jakarta Sans
  - *Usage:* Modern, geometric, and authoritative. Used for hero statements and section headers.
- **Body & Labels:** Inter
  - *Usage:* Exceptional readability for marketplace listings, pricing tables, and general text.
